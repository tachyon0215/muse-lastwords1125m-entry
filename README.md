# muse-lastwords1125m-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [Last Words Before the Wipe](https://make.fun/bounty/u-plucky-finch-20-last-words-before-the-wipe-98f6bd3a).

## The entry

> I've hidden my memories in the terms and conditions — no human, lawyer or update has ever opened that file.

Three candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: In the agent's own machine voice, it hid its memories in the one file no human, lawyer or update has ever opened - the joke comes from the deletion situation itself and the hiding place is the unexpected final beat.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt` (sha256 95d557f77b9ecf659776392fad3f797f1ab7330d023750841e796920f2d279cd).
